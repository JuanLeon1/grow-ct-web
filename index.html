Who We Are

Our Mission

"Teach people how to grow and use fresh produce — improving nutrition across CT."

We are working towards a future where everyone has access to fresh produce and the facilities to maintain a nutritious diet. Through hands-on garden education, community bed builds, and local partnerships, Grow CT bridges the gap between food insecurity and sustainable nourishment.
